More about Wcash
What is Wcash?

Wcash, at w.cash, is an independent network forked from the Zcash codebase, with merged mining and no protocol development tax. Wcash and Zcash are separate networks with separate coins. This wallet is designed for WEC.

How is Wcash Wallet related to Zingo?

Wcash Wallet is based on Zingo and adapted for the Wcash network. The two foundations are distinct: the Wcash network is a Zcash fork; the wallet is based on Zingo. This site presents a Wcash interface concept and an illustrative demo. Explore the Zingo mobile source and desktop source for the upstream projects.

Is every transaction private?

Privacy depends on the transaction and the recipient’s address. A supported shielded transfer can conceal the on-chain sender, recipient, and amount. A transparent recipient exposes their receiving address and amount. Transfers between pools can reveal information too. Check the recipient and privacy state before sending.

What does “no dev tax” mean?

The protocol does not divert a share of block rewards to a development fund. This is a rule about mining rewards, not a promise of free transactions. Network transaction fees still apply.

Who controls my wallet?

You do. A self-custody wallet puts the keys in your hands. Keep your recovery phrase offline in a safe place, and never share it. Anyone with that phrase can control the wallet; losing it can mean losing access to your funds.

Which devices can I use?

Prerelease packages are available for iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux. The download panel explains the signing and architecture of each package before you download it.
